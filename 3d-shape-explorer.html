👩‍🏫 Teacher & Facilitator Guide

Core Pedagogical Principle: Direct property tables are hidden until students physically rotate, inspect, count, and input their own observations.

Classroom Discovery Sequence:

  1. SEE & ROTATE: Have students rotate high quality SVG 3D models using smooth drag gestures to realize 3D objects have hidden sides.
  2. TOUCH & COLOR FACES: Allow students to tap individual faces with different colors to paint each face and count surfaces easily without double counting!
  3. COUNT & WRITE: Students count faces, edges, and vertices on screen and type their answers before checking.
  4. VISUAL PROOF: Clicking "Highlight Faces/Edges/Vertices" runs an animated visual pulse on each 3D element.
  5. REAL-LIFE CONNECTION: Have students classify real-world items or add objects from their classroom.
