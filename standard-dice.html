🎲 Real Dice Connection
Now pick up a real physical dice and test what you have learned!
  1. Pick any number on your real dice (e.g. 2).
  2. Predict: Before looking, calculate what number should be on the opposite side. (Hint: Remember the sum rule!)
  3. Turn the dice over and check if your prediction is correct!
  4. Try with all 3 opposite pairs:
    • 1 ↔️ 6
    • 2 ↔️ 5
    • 3 ↔️ 4
✨ Standard Dice Secret: Opposite faces ALWAYS add up to 7! (1+6=7, 2+5=7, 3+4=7)
