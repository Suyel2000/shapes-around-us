      Teacher Guide & How to Explore

      Pedagogical Model: OBSERVE → THINK → ANSWER → CHECK → DISCOVER


      This TLM avoids giving learners pre-written shape facts immediately. Instead, students are prompted to count sides and vertices, test angle guides, rotate shapes, and solve clues to construct spatial knowledge actively.


      Classroom Usage Tips:

      • Use on smart interactive whiteboards for whole-class discovery.
      • Encourage pairs to discuss before clicking "CHECK ANSWER".
      • Use the Comparison Lab and Mystery Shape Challenge to build mathematical reasoning.
