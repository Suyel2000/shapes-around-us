🧩 Pattern Blocks Guide

Welcome to Pattern Blocks! Learn geometry, fractions, and symmetry through hands-on virtual block manipulation.

🎮 How to Controls:

  • Add Blocks: Click or drag shapes from the left sidebar onto the workspace.
  • Move & Snap: Drag blocks with mouse or finger. Blocks automatically snap magnetically along matching edges!
  • Rotate: Click a block to select it, then drag the top blue rotation handle or click rotation buttons in toolbar.
  • Pan & Zoom: Scroll wheel zooms in/out. Drag empty canvas space to pan around.

🍰 Educational Modes:

  • Creative: Free-play design canvas with snap grid.
  • Fractions: Real-time visual fraction breakdown relative to Hexagon (1 Whole).
  • Symmetry: Mirror line guides for reflective pattern creation.
  • Puzzles: Solve silhouette shapes with automatic snap verification and celebration rewards!
  • Geometry: Detailed shape properties, side angles, perimeter, and mathematical relationships.
